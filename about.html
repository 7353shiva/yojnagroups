YOJNA GROUP
WHO WE ARE

A Group With Different Possibilities.

Yojna was established on January-24th, 2004 by its parent company Industrial Care, a well-known consultancy group operating across India since 1987. The main motto of Yojna is to provide "Customised Professional Service" to its customers.

With product promotions becoming essential for companies looking to showcase their products, Industrial Care established Yojna Events on January-25th, 2004 to cater to these needs. The division's motto is to deliver "Customised Professional Service" in event management.

Yojna Events has executed projects for companies like Tata Indicom, Jetking, HDFC, Hero Honda, JCB, and more. It conducts events across Karnataka and is steadily expanding elsewhere.

Creating experiences, connecting people and turning possibilities into reality.
