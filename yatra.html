ABOUT YOJNA YATRA

Making Travel More Convenient.

Yojna Yatra focuses on providing practical travel support for individuals, families, groups and organisations.

Our travel activities include air, road and rail ticket bookings, package tours and taxis on hire.

Whether it is planning a journey, arranging transportation or organising travel requirements, the objective is to make the travel experience simple and convenient.
