ABOUT YOJNA EVENTS

Professional Events. Purposeful Experiences.

With product promotions becoming an important requirement for companies looking for new ways to showcase their products, Industrial Care identified the need for a professional event management company.

Yojna Events was established on January 25th, 2004 by its parent company Industrial Care.

Industrial Care was established in 1987 and has operations across India. Yojna Events was created with the objective of providing "Customised Professional Service" in the field of event management.

Over the years, Yojna Events has executed projects for companies including Tata Indicom, Jetking, HDFC, Hero Honda and JCB, among others.
