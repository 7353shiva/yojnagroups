ABOUT YOJNA EVENTS

Your Event, Our Execution.

Yojna Events is the event management division of Yojna Group — built to serve companies that need professional, on-ground brand experiences. As product promotions became essential for reaching customers, we stepped in to handle every stage of an event, from planning to final execution.

Our approach is simple: understand the objective first, then design the right experience around it. Whether it's a product launch, roadshow, exhibition, conference, or corporate gathering, every event is planned with clear coordination, creative direction, and dependable on-ground execution.

Over the years, Yojna Events has delivered work for brands like Tata Indicom, Jetking, HDFC, Hero Honda, and JCB — across Karnataka and beyond. Each project reinforces our core motto: “Customised Professional Service” in event management.
